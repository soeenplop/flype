Set up Flype on iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Flype and follow the setup. It guides you through every step.
  2. Choose the speech engine: download Parakeet (about 632 MB, Wi-Fi recommended) or use Apple speech recognition.
  3. Add the keyboard under General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard and allow Full Access for voice input.
  4. Allow microphone access when Flype asks.
  5. In a text field, choose Flype with the globe key and tap the microphone next to the space bar.

The app has a test field and further help. If its menus are in German, you can change the app language in Settings (“Einstellungen”).

Set up Flype on Mac

  1. Open Flype and allow the microphone and Accessibility during setup. Reopen the app afterwards if needed.
  2. Select a text field, for example in Notes.
  3. Hold the right ⌘ key, speak, and let go to insert the text.

You can set a different microphone key in Settings. Flype requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.

Common problems on iPhone and iPad

There's no microphone in the keyboard, or it doesn't respond

Check that Full Access is on for the Flype keyboard. Without it you can type, but not use voice input.

The Flype app opens when I tap the microphone

That happens the first time, because iOS doesn't give keyboards access to the microphone. Switch back to your app afterwards. You start and stop further recordings right in the keyboard.

The microphone turned off again

Readiness ends when you lock the device, turn it off in Flype or no Flype keyboard is active for five minutes. Just tap the microphone again.

The Flype keyboard doesn't appear in a field

iOS always uses the system keyboard in password fields. Individual apps can also exclude third-party keyboards.

A name keeps being written wrong

Add it with the spelling you want to your words in the app.

Common problems on Mac

Nothing happens when I hold the key

Check under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility that Flype is allowed, then reopen the app. A very short press deliberately doesn't start a recording.

The recording gets cancelled

Pressing another key or clicking in another app while holding cancels the recording, so that regular shortcuts keep working. A recording ends after two minutes, and locking the Mac or putting it to sleep cancels it.

The text wasn't inserted

If you switched app or window or typed in the meantime, Flype doesn't insert. It also skips protected password fields. Your latest text is ready to copy on Flype's home screen.

Delete your account

In the app, open your account (“Dein Konto” in German) and choose to delete it (“Konto löschen”). This permanently removes the account, words and settings; local words and settings stay on the device. The privacy policy explains what else happens to your data.
