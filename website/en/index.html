iPhone & iPad

Speak a thought.
Keep writing.

A quick reply, a shopping list or a longer email: Flype combines speaking and typing in one keyboard. Speak your text, then edit a word or keep typing right where you are.

  • In the app you're using. Your words appear in the active text field. You decide when to send them.
  • A familiar keyboard. Letters, word suggestions and accented characters stay within reach.
  • A guided start. Flype walks you through setting up the keyboard and microphone. The home screen shows when you're ready.

For iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 26 or later.

See the setup guide

Mac

Hold.
Speak. Let go.

Put an email into words without switching apps. Hold the right Command key (⌘), speak and let go. Flype inserts the text at your cursor, ready for you to review.

  • Your key. Set a microphone key that feels right for you.
  • Your workflow. Your usual keyboard shortcuts keep working.
  • Your text. If insertion doesn't work, your latest text is ready to copy in Flype.

For Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.

See the Mac setup guide

Why Flype exists

I can't type as fast as I speak. But I also want to edit the text by hand afterwards.

On iPhone that usually means speaking in one app and correcting with a different keyboard. Many voice input apps don't offer a QWERTZ keyboard with a German layout at all, and for what they do they're often relatively expensive.

So I built the keyboard I wanted for myself: umlauts where they belong, a microphone next to the space bar and speech recognition that runs on the device. The Mac app came next, so my words and settings are the same on all my devices.

Sören Prilop, developer of Flype

How it works on iPhone and iPad

  1. 1

    Tap the microphone

    It sits next to the space bar. The first time, Flype opens briefly, because iOS doesn't let keyboards use the microphone themselves. Then you switch back.

  2. 2

    Speak and stop

    The keys make way for a round stop button that reacts to your voice. A recording can last up to two minutes, and you start the next ones right from the keyboard.

  3. 3

    The text is in the field

    Flype inserts it where you're writing and doesn't send anything. The keys are back right away.

What's inside

Your voice stays on your device

Speech becomes text on your device. After the first download, it works without internet. Your recording stays on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Your words

Save names and terms you use often. Your dictionary helps Flype find the spelling you want.

Six languages

Set the language for menus and for voice input separately. On iPhone and iPad the key layout follows the language: QWERTZ, QWERTY or AZERTY.

  • German QWERTZ
  • English QWERTY
  • French AZERTY
  • Italian QWERTY
  • Spanish QWERTY with ñ
  • Dutch QWERTY

One dictionary on all your devices

With an optional account you sync words and settings between iPhone, iPad and Mac. Without one, everything stays local.

  • Sentences you started

    Flype continues from what you've already typed, on all your devices.

  • Word suggestions

    Three suggestions above the keys, computed locally and only applied when you tap them.

  • Accents

    Long-press a key for accented characters. The German layout has Ü, Ö and Ä directly on the keyboard.

  • iPad

    Wide iPads add a number row and more function keys.

What stays where

Your recordings stay on your device. Account sync and cloud cleanup are optional. Flype has no ads or tracking.

Read the privacy policy
Voice recordings
Only ever on your device. They are deleted after processing or when you cancel.
Recognized text
On your device, with no history. The latest text stays in memory until you delete it, record again or quit Flype.
What you type
Not stored. Flype only counts unknown words on the device to learn them. When a recording starts, Flype reads up to 200 characters before and after the cursor so the text fits the sentence, and it deletes them afterwards.
Accessibility on Mac
Only for the microphone key and inserting text. It lets Flype detect your key and write into the active field. Flype skips protected password fields.
Cleanup
Off until you turn it on. It runs in the cloud and requires an account and your consent. The text, never the recording, then goes to OpenAI for cleanup.
Account
Optional. It stores your email address, words and settings for sync. You can delete it in the app at any time.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Flype cost?

We'll announce the price at launch. Flype is meant to stay affordable, which was one of the reasons for building it.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.

Where do I get the Mac app?

When Flype launches, the Mac download will be available here. The iPhone and iPad app will be in the App Store. Neither download is available yet.

Does Flype work offline?

Yes. You only need to download the speech model once: Parakeet is about 632 MB, and Apple speech recognition fetches its language data through the system when needed. After that, recognition works without internet.

What do I need for cleanup?

A Flype account and your consent, because cleanup runs with an AI model from OpenAI in the cloud. Without it, Flype inserts the recognized text verbatim. Speech recognition itself always runs on your device.

Why does the app open on iPhone when I tap the microphone?

iOS doesn't give keyboards access to the microphone, so recording starts in the Flype app. That only happens when you tap the microphone, and once it's running you start and stop further recordings right in the keyboard. Readiness ends when you lock the device, turn it off in Flype or no Flype keyboard is active for five minutes.

Why does Flype need Full Access on iPhone?

So the keyboard and app can exchange the recognized text and the keyboard can read your dictionary. Regular typing works without Full Access, voice input doesn't.

Why does Flype need Accessibility on Mac?

Otherwise macOS doesn't let an app detect a key in other programs or insert text there. Flype also asks for the microphone. Separate Input Monitoring isn't needed.

Do I need an account?

No. You only need one for syncing between devices and for the optional cleanup. You then sign in with Apple or Google.

Does Flype work in every app?

In almost every text field. iOS always switches to the system keyboard in password fields, and some apps exclude third-party keyboards. On Mac, Flype doesn't insert if you switched app or window in the meantime; your text then stays in Flype, ready to copy.
