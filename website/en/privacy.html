What stays on your device

Voice recordings and the text recognized from them are processed on your device. This applies to both speech engines, Parakeet and Apple speech recognition, and to the optional cleanup unless you turn on cloud cleanup. Recordings are deleted after processing or when you cancel. There is no transcription history. Your last text stays in memory only until you delete it, transcribe again or quit the app.

The Flype keyboard does not store your keystrokes. Only when a transcription starts does Flype read up to 200 characters before the cursor, so the recognized text fits the sentence you started. They are deleted after the transcription and leave your device only with cloud cleanup turned on. The keyboard needs Full Access to receive transcriptions from the app and to read your personal dictionary. Regular typing works without Full Access.

Accessibility on Mac

The Mac app needs the Accessibility permission to detect your microphone key in other programs and insert text into the active field. It evaluates other keys only to cancel a transcription when you press a key combination; it does not store keystrokes. When a transcription starts it reads up to 200 characters before the cursor in the active field, deleted as described above. It skips protected password fields. To insert text it briefly places it on the clipboard and then restores the previous contents.

Microphone

Flype records only after you start a recording. On iPhone and iPad the microphone stays active after the first transcription for further transcriptions, without storing audio between them. iOS indicates this with the orange dot. You end it in Flype or by locking the device, or it ends by itself after five minutes without an active keyboard. On Mac, Flype records only while you hold the microphone key, for at most two minutes; locking the Mac or putting it to sleep cancels the recording.

Speech model download

When you download Parakeet, the app fetches the model from Hugging Face (Hugging Face, Inc., USA). The provider technically receives your IP address. No recordings or text are transmitted. Apple speech recognition downloads its language data through the operating system when needed.

Cloud cleanup (optional)

Cloud cleanup is off by default and requires an account. If you turn it on, each transcription to be cleaned up is sent, along with up to 200 characters before the cursor and matching terms from your words, through our processor Supabase to OpenAI, where an AI model processes it. According to OpenAI, data sent through the API is not used for training and is retained for up to 30 days for abuse detection. Voice recordings are never transmitted.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by turning the feature off. Flype does not store the text. For the usage limit we store only the number of requests and characters per account; these counters are deleted with the account.

Account and sync

An account is optional. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we process the following to provide sync (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):

  • email address and user ID
  • your personal words and their variants
  • your settings for writing style, languages, microphone key and speech engine

This data is held by our processor Supabase Inc. Open: add project region and transfer mechanism. Recordings and transcriptions are not synced. Sign-in is handled by Apple or Google, who process data under their own terms. Your sign-in token is stored in your device's protected keychain.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data until you delete your account, which you can do at any time in the app under “Dein Konto” (Your account). This permanently removes the account, words and settings, and for Apple sign-in revokes the link to your Apple ID. Local words and settings remain on each device.

Moving the Mac app to the Trash does not automatically delete local files (including speech models), settings or the sign-in in the keychain. When you delete the iPhone or iPad app, iOS removes its local files; a sign-in in the keychain may remain. Please sign out in Flype before removing the app.

No ads and no tracking

Flype contains no advertising, no tracking and no analytics services.

This website

This website sets no cookies, loads no external fonts or services and contains no analytics. The keyboard demo runs only in your browser and records nothing; what you type there is not transmitted.

When you visit, our hosting provider processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time and requested page in order to deliver and secure the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Open: add hosting provider, server location and log retention.
